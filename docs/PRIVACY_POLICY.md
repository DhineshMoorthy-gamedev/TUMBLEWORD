1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This Privacy Policy applies to the game "Rotatle" across all supported platforms 
and distribution channels, including:
  • Mobile Applications (Google Play Store / Android)
  • Web Editions (Hosted on Netlify / Modern Web Browsers)
  • Desktop & Standalone Editions (Itch.io / Standalone Builds)
  • Any related services operated by OLAI Digital Studios ("we", "us", or "our").

OLAI Digital Studios is committed to protecting the privacy of our players. 
Rotatle is designed with an offline-first architecture to provide an enjoyable, 
distraction-free, and privacy-respecting gravity word puzzle experience. We do 
not collect, sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information (PII).


2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rotatle does NOT require mandatory account creation, login, or social media 
authentication. We do not collect, harvest, track, or store:
  • Real names, physical home addresses, or telephone numbers
  • Email addresses (unless you voluntarily contact our customer support team)
  • Address books, contacts, camera, photos, or microphone data
  • Precise geolocation or GPS coordinates
  • Biometric or sensitive personal data
  • Bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, or financial account credentials


3. LOCAL ON-DEVICE STORAGE (GAMEPLAY PROGRESS & SETTINGS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rotatle stores your gameplay progression and user preferences exclusively on your 
local device using standard local storage technologies (such as HTML5 localStorage 
and isolated application sandboxes). This local data includes:
  • Campaign progression across all dynamic grid levels (N = 5, 6, 7, 8 dimensions)
  • Multi-word gauntlet completion status, optimal move records, and star ratings
  • Daily Challenge completion history, live streak counters, and calendar stats
  • Total words solved counter and best streak records
  • Player preferences: Sound effects state, Haptic vibration state, Daily Reminder toggle,
    and Visual themes (Obsidian Dark / Vellum Paper)
  • In-app purchase entitlement status (e.g., Ad-Free mode state)

This data remains strictly on your local device and is NEVER transmitted to external 
cloud servers or third-party databases. You may reset or erase your data at any time 
by selecting "Reset All Progress" / "Clear Data" in the in-game Settings menu or by 
clearing the app's storage and cache in your Android or browser device settings.


4. LOCAL DEVICE NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rotatle provides an optional "Daily Reminder" feature that alerts you to new Daily 
Challenge puzzles.
  • These notifications are generated locally on your device by the operating system 
    at a user-configured time (default: 10:00 AM).
  • No external push notification servers, device tokens, or remote tracking systems 
    are used.
  • You can enable, disable, or modify reminder permissions at any time directly within 
    the in-game Settings menu or through your device's System Notification settings.
