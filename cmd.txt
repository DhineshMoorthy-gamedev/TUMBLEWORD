Yes, exactly right! Here is a quick reference for both:
──────
### 1. Google Play Store (AdMob Production Access)

• What you need to do: Once your AdMob account and app review are approved for production ads, just let me know (or you can
do it anytime).
• What gets restored:
    1. In admobConfig.js, we set isTesting: false.
    2. In AndroidManifest.xml, we restore your production App ID from cmd.txt (ca-app-pub-4890313674539327~7104584458).
    3. Run npm run build:playstore and generate your signed Release AAB.

──────
### 2. Microsoft Store (Product Identity)

• What you need to do:
    1. Go to Microsoft Partner Center https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard.
    2. Reserve your product name (e.g. Rotatle).
    3. In the left navigation of your app, go to Product management > Product Identity.
    4. Copy these 3 values and paste them here:
        • Package/Identity/Name (e.g. 12345OlaiDigitalStudios.Rotatle)
        • Package/Identity/Publisher (e.g. CN=A1B2C3D4-5678-90AB-CDEF-1234567890AB)
        • Package/Properties/PublisherDisplayName (e.g. Olai Digital Studios)

• What gets updated:
    • We paste those into bundle.config.json and tauri.conf.json.
    • Run npm run tauri:msix.
    • The resulting .msixbundle will match Microsoft Partner Center's identity check, ready to drag-and-drop into the  
    submission package uploader.

  │ "Update to version 0.0.17 (versionCode 17) and build Android AAB"
