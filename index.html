How to Play

1. Turn Gravity: Tap Turn Left or Turn Right (or use A / D / Arrow keys) to rotate the maze $90^\circ$. All letters slide and fall down.

2. Bond Words in Order: Slide consecutive letters next to each other in correct word order (e.g., C + A $\rightarrow$ CA, then CA + T $\rightarrow$ CAT).

3. Completed Words Tumble: When a word is formed, it becomes a solid obstacle tile that stays on the board, altering gravity for future words!

4. Clear the Level: Complete all words in the sequence to conquer the level.
