Privacy Policy

Rotatle is built with a strict offline-first, zero-data-collection philosophy by OLAI Digital Studios.

1. Zero Personal Data Collected

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, contacts, photos, camera, or precise geolocation data.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All puzzle progress (dynamic N=5, 6, 7, 8 grids), completed words, streaks, optimal move scores, and visual preferences are stored exclusively on your device via standard local storage.

3. In-App Purchases & Monetization

In-app purchases (Remove Ads) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. OLAI Digital Studios does not receive, handle, or store any financial credentials.

4. Local Device Notifications

Daily Challenge reminders are generated locally on your device without external push servers or tracking tokens.
